# AR-0205 plan — Fatal coverage for BodyPost and a typed EvalError::Fatal

Milestone: [M2](../milestones/M2.md). Task: [AR-0205](../tasks/AR-0205.md).

## Scope

Findings of AR-0106: (1) the four BodyPost kernels trace zero fatal slots even though
`Motor::inverse` divides, so a degenerate motor reaching BodyPost is never flagged; (2) the host
reports a tripped fatal slot as `EvalError::Backend`, while `EvalError::Fatal { shader, sites }` in
crates/newton/src/error.rs is defined and unused.

Deliver:
1. Find why the BodyPost trace carries no fatal operand for the inverse's division (pruned at
   trace time? guard missing in the CPU source?) and make the guard survive into the kernel.
   Generated kernels other than BodyPost must be byte-identical; record a diff summary.
2. Report a tripped fatal slot as `EvalError::Fatal` naming the shader and the slot(s), and keep
   `Backend` for transport failures. Update callers that match on the variant.
3. A test drives a degenerate motor into BodyPost and observes `EvalError::Fatal`, batch-mates
   unaffected. AR-0201's oracle must still pass.
