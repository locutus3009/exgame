# AR-0204 plan — Place a body at the island anchor's full precision

Milestone: [M2](../milestones/M2.md). Task: [AR-0204](../tasks/AR-0204.md).

## Scope

Finding of AR-0107 (crates/newton/tests/precision_seam.rs): with the fixed-point island anchor
(`Fix<I96F32>`), kernel inputs are offset-independent and shape is bit-identical at 1e3, 1e6 and
1e9. But there is no public way to place a body at the anchor's precision: a body is constructed
from an absolute f32 pose, so its position is rounded at construction — up to one f32 ulp at the
offset, and at 1e9 a 0.3-spaced chain collapses to one point.

Deliver a construction path that takes a position in the anchor's precision (or a high-precision
origin plus an f32 local pose) and places the body without passing its absolute position through
f32. Keep existing constructors working. Extend `precision_seam.rs`'s ingestion case to use the
new path and assert that the error at 1e9 is now bounded by the f32 precision of the LOCAL offset,
not of the absolute position.
