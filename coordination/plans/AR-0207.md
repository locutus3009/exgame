# AR-0207 plan — A first physically consistent configuration of the star system

Milestone: [M2](../milestones/M2.md). Task: [AR-0207](../tasks/AR-0207.md).

## Scope

docs/architecture/planned/setting.md sketches the game's star system and says its physically
consistent configuration (masses, semi-major axes, eccentricities, long-term stability, the gas
giant's moon resonances, the habitable moon's heat budget under a dimmer primary) is still to be
designed.

Deliver a FIRST configuration, small enough to integrate in a test: the star, the gas giant, the
habitable moon and at least one other moon of the giant (more planets optional). Choose parameters
from first principles, state each with its reason (Hill-sphere and Roche checks for the moons, a
habitable-zone estimate for the giant's orbit, a resonance or spacing argument between moons), and
record the table in setting.md as a proposal, keeping that document's planned status. Provide it
as a reusable scenario constructor in newton so the flight example can load it. A test integrates
it for many orbits of the habitable moon and asserts semi-major axes and eccentricities stay
within stated bounds. Units and the time step are stated. This is content design under physical
constraint; flag every choice that is the owner's taste rather than physics.
