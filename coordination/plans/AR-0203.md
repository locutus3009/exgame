# AR-0203 plan — Analytic orbit checks: Kepler two-body period, eccentricity, apsides

Milestone: [M2](../milestones/M2.md). Task: [AR-0203](../tasks/AR-0203.md).

## Scope

Deferred from M0 (docs/QUALITY.md): Kepler and Lagrange-point analytic checks. This AR takes
Kepler only; Lagrange points are left to a later milestone because they need a three-body setup and
a stability criterion that deserves its own plan.

Deliver one test file that integrates a two-body orbit through the gravity propagator for a
circular orbit and at least two eccentricities (e.g. 0.3 and 0.7), and asserts:
- orbital period within a stated relative tolerance of 2π·sqrt(a³/μ);
- eccentricity and periapsis/apoapsis distances within stated tolerances over several orbits;
- apsidal precession per orbit below a measured bound (a pure Kepler problem does not precess).
State the step size and how the tolerances follow from it. A failure is a finding: record it and
ask for an AR rather than loosening the tolerance.
