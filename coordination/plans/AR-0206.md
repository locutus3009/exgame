Milestone: [M2](../milestones/M2.md). Task: [AR-0206](../tasks/AR-0206.md).

## Scope

Known drift at the close of M1:
- docs/QUALITY.md "What is deferred" still lists the two `FIXME: deadlock?` sites (fixed by
  AR-0105) and, after M2, will list physics-invariant tests and the CPU-versus-GPU oracle.
- crates/hitchcock/src/camera.rs ignored soak tests carry comments about a "CPU backend only" and
  being "too slow under Lua"; there is no Lua backend.
- docs/architecture/decided/single-scalar-type.md demands one scalar type at every boundary, and
  docs/architecture/open/fixed-point-vs-f64.md leans to `f64`. The owner's decision (confirmed
  2026-10-02) is the split the code implements: `f32` for local physics, fixed-point for the
  reference frames themselves. Record that split as a decided document, mark both old documents
  superseded by it (keeping their content as history, the way open/no-floating-origin.md does),
  and update the index and every reference.
Deliver: correct every such claim, citing the AR that changed it; move built items out of the
deferred list into the enforced or tested section with the command that backs them; leave
genuinely deferred items (determinism gate, performance budgets) in place. Comment-only changes
in crates/.
