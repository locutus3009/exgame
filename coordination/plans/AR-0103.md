# AR-0103 plan — Cross-mechanism epoch driver with frozen structure and quiescence flush

Milestone: [M1](../milestones/M1.md). Task: [AR-0103](../tasks/AR-0103.md).

## Scope

ACCELERATOR.md Part II: the driver steps all mechanisms of an epoch concurrently, so batch
breadth comes from many mechanisms; the epoch invariant (structure frozen within an epoch, no body
shared across mechanisms) makes the traversal order-independent; and the flush backstop is
quiescence — when every stepping mechanism is parked on the accelerator, flush.

Deliver:
1. A driver type in newton that owns or references a set of mechanisms sharing one accelerator
   and one World, and steps all of them for one epoch concurrently.
2. Structure frozen within an epoch: a structural mutation (add_body, connect, remove_body,
   split, merge) on a mechanism while its epoch is in progress is refused with an error, not
   queued silently and not deadlocked.
3. A body may not belong to two mechanisms: adding a body already owned elsewhere is refused.
4. Quiescence flush: the accelerator knows how many producers are active in the epoch and how
   many are parked on a submit, and flushes when they are equal, in addition to batch-full. Keep
   the existing idle path working for callers that do not use the driver.
5. Migrate the hand-rolled join_all callers listed in the owned paths to the driver. melies
   examples must compile but must not be launched.

## Acceptance criteria

- A test steps several mechanisms through the driver and matches the trajectories of the same mechanisms stepped alone, bit-exactly.
- A test attempts a structural mutation mid-epoch and observes the refusal.
- A test adds one body to two mechanisms and observes the refusal.
- Flush counts per step on `cloth_grid_stability` before and after are recorded in evidence; the change must not increase them.
- `cargo build --workspace --release --all-targets` and `cargo test --workspace --release` pass.
