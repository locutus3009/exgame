# AR-0107 plan — Precision seam: measure f32 base poses far from the origin

Milestone: [M1](../milestones/M1.md). Task: [AR-0107](../tasks/AR-0107.md).

## Scope

The kernels read base-pose Motors as f32. Islands keep an `S` origin re-anchored to the centre of
mass each step (crates/newton/src/mechanism/mechanism.rs around 359 and 388; island.rs around 179
and 206), which should make the shader inputs small regardless of world position.

Deliver a test, in a new file only, that steps one jointed mechanism (e.g. a short chain) near the
origin and the same mechanism translated by a large offset (1e3, 1e6, 1e9 world units), and
compares body positions relative to the first body after N steps. Record the observed divergence
per offset. Assert the tolerance the measurement supports. If an offset shows real loss of
precision, do not fix it here: record it and ask for a follow-up AR.
