# AR-0202 plan — Conservation property tests: momentum, angular momentum, energy drift

Milestone: [M2](../milestones/M2.md). Task: [AR-0202](../tasks/AR-0202.md).

## Scope

Deferred from M0 (docs/QUALITY.md): energy, momentum and angular-momentum drift bounds.

Deliver one test file covering, for the implicit `Newton` midpoint step and each explicit scheme
(`ExplicitEuler`, `SymplecticEuler`, `LieEuler`):
1. A free mechanism with internal joints and no external field: total linear and angular
   momentum in the world frame are conserved up to a measured bound over N steps.
2. A conservative system (spring without damping): energy drift over N steps is bounded, with
   the expectation that symplectic schemes stay bounded and explicit Euler grows — assert the
   behaviour each scheme should have, not one bound for all.
Bounds are the measured values with stated headroom. A scheme that violates what it should
conserve is a finding: record it and ask for an AR, do not weaken the assertion to pass.
